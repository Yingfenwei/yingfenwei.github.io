About

Our research spans the full chain from materials discovery and physical mechanisms to device fabrication, memory integration, and circuit demonstrations. Current interests include HfO₂- and AlScN-based ferroelectrics, high-k dielectrics, emerging non-volatile memories, and related applications for future computing technologies.

Our work has been published in leading journals and presented at premier conferences in integrated circuits, including Science, Nature Materials, VLSI and so on.

We welcome collaborations with researchers from academia and industry, as well as applications from motivated students and postdoctoral researchers.
